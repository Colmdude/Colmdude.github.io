# PRODUCT.md

**Who it's for:** people who find Coleman's photography through friends, social posts or a link: peers, creatives, anyone curious. They look on a phone first.

**What they should feel:** quiet, moody, cinematic. A dark room with the photos glowing. Nothing competes with the pictures.

**Voice:** minimal. Name, one line, the work. No marketing language.

**Look:** deep navy (#060d1f), orange (#ff8c00) for the name and labels, Barlow light/regular uppercase with wide tracking.
